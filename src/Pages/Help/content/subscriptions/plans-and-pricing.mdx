## Plans and Pricing

Game Datacards has a free tier and two paid plans. All local features are free: datacard rendering, printing, the datasource editor and local storage. The plans differ in how much you can store in the cloud.

### Subscription tiers

| Tier | Cloud Categories | Cloud Datasources | Templates | Price |
|------|------------------|--------------------|-----------|-------|
| **Free** | 2 | 1 | 2 | Free |
| <IconLabel icon="Crown" label="Premium" color="#faad14" /> | 50 | 2 | 4 | &euro;3.99/month |
| <IconLabel icon="Crown" label="Creator" color="#722ed1" /> | 250 | 10 | 10 | &euro;7.99/month |

### What each tier includes

**Free**: create and print datacards, build custom datasources and store everything locally in your browser. With a free account you can also sync 2 categories, 1 datasource and 2 designer templates to the cloud.

<Icon name="Crown" /> **Premium**: higher cloud limits. Sync up to 50 categories, 2 datasources and 4 designer templates across your devices.

<Icon name="Crown" /> **Creator**: the highest limits, for users who manage several armies or game systems. Sync up to 250 categories, 10 datasources and 10 designer templates.
