## Creating a Datasource

Click <Icon name="Plus" /> **New Datasource** in the left panel to get started. A step-by-step wizard walks you through the setup:

- **Name, version, and author**: identify your datasource so you can find it later.
- **Base system**: choose Warhammer 40K 11th Edition, 10th Edition (legacy), Age of Sigmar, or Blank. This determines which presets are available and how your cards look in preview.
- **First card type**: define the first type of card your datasource will contain.
